## Workflow

1. Launch with Launch.cmd and open the localhost dashboard. Automatic Discovery is the default for both new and existing users.
2. Click **Run Quick Scan**. No names, API keys or CSVs are necessary for the implemented public catalog routes. Optional limits bound queries, sources and observations.
3. Read opportunity cards: purchase quote, retail asking-price reference, observed difference, match uncertainty, missing costs, timestamps and next step. Unknown costs do not discard otherwise useful candidates or become zero costs.
4. Select one or more cards, click **Deep Research selected candidates**, then **Run Deep Research**. Saved candidates are also available. Without seeds, Deep Research first discovers products and uses the remaining query budget for focused follow-ups.
5. Use Guided Research for model/market/budget/profit/delivery preferences. Dropshipping additionally requires confirmed single-unit direct fulfillment; retailers are not treated as confirmed direct-fulfillment suppliers. Inventory economics include MOQ cash commitment and budget feasibility.
6. Save or dismiss findings, inspect evidence/profit breakdowns, or record a manual match decision. Known specification conflicts, minimum/coupon prices, missing costs and currency mismatches cannot be overridden into automatic profits.
7. Research sessions retain plans, completed collections and evidence. Use Streamlit Stop to interrupt, then choose that session and Resume research. Completed steps are not repeated. Recent successful snapshots are reused for 15 minutes with original timestamps. Start a new scan to retry failed collection.
8. Advanced Tools retains every original screen, CSV imports, editable cost assumptions, Google Ads evidence, manual comparisons, saved products, history comparisons, exports, source settings and diagnostics.

## Limits and security

Deep Research performs focused catalog follow-ups and evaluates reported specifications, stock, prices, shipping, MOQ and destination evidence. It does not automate variant selectors, logins or checkout shipping quotes. These remain explicit supplier investigation tasks; no shipping eligibility, final unit cost or fulfillment promise is fabricated. AliExpress minimum/coupon prices and PAB currency cannot establish automatic USD profit. Retail asking prices do not establish sales or resale feasibility.

No new retailer is claimed as verified. CJ, Walmart, Target, eBay and Alibaba retain their existing access/extraction limitations and explanations in Source Manager. Existing Google Ads evidence associations are integrated; live Google authorization was not available and is not claimed as tested. No image similarity, AI runtime, forex conversion, buying, listing or advertising execution was introduced.
